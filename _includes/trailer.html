{% comment %} A book trailer, click-to-load. At rest it's a plain link to the YouTube watch page wrapped around a local poster, so nothing from YouTube loads until a reader asks for it, and it still works with JS off. On click the script swaps in the privacy-enhanced (youtube-nocookie) player and starts it — playback is reader-initiated, so there's no reduced-motion conflict. Params: id (YouTube video id), title (book title), poster (path under the site), duration (human-readable, e.g. "1 min 11 s"). {% endcomment %}
